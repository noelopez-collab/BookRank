💡 Why this recommendation?

BookRank compares the selected genre using ratings, popularity, and edition counts. The sliders change how much each factor contributes to the final score.

◈ Model transparency

  • Ratings, popularity, and editions are indicators of reader interest.
  • Open Library data can be incomplete or change over time.
  • Scores are normalized to compare different measures.
  • A high score does not guarantee that you will enjoy a book.
